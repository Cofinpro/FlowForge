## Product-Methodik: Plausibilität & Freigabe

_Ort: notebook:The Product - Business Design_

_Footnotes name the notebook source. The verbatim quotes and FAQ references stay with the generation output (`knowledge/`), which is not installed._

**Task:** "Betroffene Stories zur Anpassung markieren" (PO). Marks stories touched by the new story; the workflow
writes proposals and does not change the backlog.

**Procedure**

1. Impact analysis (Auswirkungsanalyse) over the traceability links: from the new or changed story find the
   artifacts that realise or relate to it [^1]. Useful relations between stories: refines, generalises,
   replaces [^2].
2. Where no traceability information exists, ask domain experts and the development team about the
   consequences [^3].
3. Mark each affected story for adjustment, e.g. review status "in Korrektur" [^4].
4. Record on each marked story (derived from the change-request template):
   - **what changes**, as the contrast to today: "Whereas currently ..." / "Instead of ..." [^5];
   - **why** (Begründung) [^6];
   - **who is responsible** and, where a change board exists, its decision and status of the impact
     analysis [^7];
   - **new version**: small change raises the increment, larger change the version (v1.0 -> v1.1) [^8][^9].
5. A story that is replaced gets the relation "replaces" and an explicit state with the reason; rejected or not
   implemented items stay documented so they are not proposed again [^2][^10][^11].
6. Time-sensitive stories carry a "best before" date [^12].

**Checklist**

- Impact traced in both directions (sources/goals above, tests/components below) [^2].
- Each mark states what, why, who decides, version.
- Nothing is deleted silently; the reason is recorded [^10][^11].

**Pitfalls**

- Stale documents left active cause confusion; documents nobody updates should be archived as history [^13].
- Silent changes: "Dokumentiere Änderungen und Entscheidungen so, dass alle Beteiligten verstehen, warum etwas
  angepasst wurde." [^14]
- Over-marking: record only traceability information with a defined use [^15]; numbering cards adds
  "pointless overhead" [^16].

**Not answered by the notebook:** no source defines a status literally named "superseded"; the sources give
the relation "ersetzt", the review status "in Korrektur" and the "Won't" category.
