## Product-Methodik: Plausibilität & Freigabe

_Ort: notebook:The Product - Business Design_

_Footnotes name the notebook source. The verbatim quotes and FAQ references stay with the generation output (`knowledge/`), which is not installed._

**Task:** "Plausibilitätsbefund zusammenführen" (PO). Merges the four parallel checks (duplicates, flow consistency,
dependencies, acceptance criteria) into one report that feeds "Konsistent mit dem übrigen Backlog?".

**Procedure**

1. Collect all findings and consolidate: merge duplicate findings, drop false findings (a reviewer's wrong
   assumption or misreading), document the rest in one findings list with corrective actions [^1].
2. Where reviewers contradict each other, identify and analyse the conflict (factual, interest, value,
   relationship, structural) before resolving it, and document the resolution [^2][^3].
3. Resolve by agreement on facts, compromise, voting, decision matrix; hierarchical decision only if the
   others fail [^4][^5]. Comparing independent results in a time-boxed "diverge and merge" exposes
   misunderstandings [^6].
4. Derive the verdict against criteria fixed beforehand [^7].

**What the report lists**

- Story id/version and the affected items (linked stories, flows, criteria); each finding with ID, source
  perspective, classification, criticality and status [^8][^9].
- Quality aspect per finding: content, documentation, agreement [^10].
- Status values: review (ungeprüft, in Prüfung, überprüft, fehlerhaft, in Korrektur) and agreement (nicht
  abgestimmt, abgestimmt, konfliktär) [^9].
- Open points that cannot be clarified directly go on an open-points list (OPL) [^11].

**Verdict**

- Release only when the story meets the agreed quality criteria: consistent (no contradiction with other
  requirements), testable, feasible, agreed [^7][^12].
- Rework: reviewers only document findings; correction is a separate step, status "in Korrektur" [^13].
- Reject/drop (e.g. unresolved duplicate): record the reason; rejected items stay documented as "Won't" [^14].

**Pitfalls**

- Shying away from conflict with the requester, or not questioning incoming requirements [^15].
- Not answered by the notebook: the concrete risks of leaving a conflict resolution undocumented.
