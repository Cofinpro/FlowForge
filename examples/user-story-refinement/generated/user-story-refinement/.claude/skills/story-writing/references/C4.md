## Product-Methodik: Story formulieren

_Ort: notebook:The Product - Business Design_

_Footnotes name the notebook source. The verbatim quotes and FAQ references stay with the generation output (`knowledge/`), which is not installed._

**Task: "INVEST-Selbstcheck durchführen"** (feeds the gateway "INVEST erfüllt?"; on failure the story is reformulated). INVEST is Bill Wake's checklist: Independent, Negotiable, Valuable, Estimatable, Small, Testable [^1].

### Checklist, with the fix per failed criterion

| Criterion | Pass question | Fail sign | Fix (as the sources give it) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Independent | Can it be built and shipped in any order? | A cannot be coded or tested before B; estimates depend on order [^2] | Combine the dependent stories [^3]; or split differently; or put two estimates on the card (done first / done second) [^4] |
| Negotiable | Is the card an invitation to talk, not a spec? | UI/technical detail on the card | Keep only the story and questions on the front [^5]; add a "Whereas currently…" clause to focus the talk [^6] |
| Valuable | Does a user or purchaser get value? | Developer-internal wording ("connection pool", "refactor Login class") | Rewrite as the user outcome [^7]; the customer side writes it |
| Estimable | Can developers estimate it? | "No idea how long"; missing domain or technical knowledge | Time-boxed spike as its own story [^8]; split learning from earning [^9] |
| Small | Fits one iteration? | Epic; estimate above velocity | Split into end-to-end slices [^10]; merge tiny stories. Cohn's range: "between half a day and perhaps two weeks" [^11] |
| Testable | Are there objective pass/fail criteria? | "easy to use", "never wait long" [^12] | Tests on the back of the card [^13]; system-wide limits as constraint cards [^14] |

### Conflicting criteria

Independent and valuable often conflict with small [^15]. Do not buy smallness with value by splitting along technical layers (form vs. database) [^16]. Quantify the behaviour change instead, so a smaller subset stays valuable ("import contacts 20% faster": a 5% speedup is still valuable) [^17].

### Not answered by the notebook

The 1-3 day size from the notebook's INVEST table is its own summary; the quoted passage gives the wider range above. The answers do not state a scoring rule (how many criteria may fail). Also see the sprint-review question "how will we demonstrate this story?" [^18].
