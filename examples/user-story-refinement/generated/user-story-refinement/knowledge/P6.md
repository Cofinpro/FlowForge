## Product-Methodik: Plausibilität & Freigabe

_Ort: notebook:The Product - Business Design_

**Task:** "Mit bestehender Story zusammenführen" (PO). The new story duplicates or overlaps an existing one; the
workflow writes a merge proposal, the existing story survives.

**Procedure**

1. Pick the surviving story. For a cluster of overlaps write the cluster's titles as bullet points under one
   header story with a title that distils why they belong together [^1][^2].
2. Carry over the new story's content into the survivor: its titles/variants as bullets, its acceptance
   criteria and examples, and the link to the originating feedback. The notebook derives this from the card
   practice above; no source states a transfer rule verbatim. (inferred)
3. Re-estimate the combined story as one unit by the team, comparing it with already estimated stories [^3][^4].
   Overlapping work reduces the effort of the second story, so the sum of the parts is not the estimate [^5].
4. Size check: a story should be codable and testable in about half a day to two weeks; a larger one is an epic
   and gets split [^6]. If the combined story is much longer, split along another dimension, as vertical
   slices through all layers, not along technical layers [^7][^8].
5. Absorbed card: propose in `09_aenderungsvorschlaege.md` that the team closes it with the reason recorded
   (consolidate overlapping cards or keep the card archived; in a digital backlog: Trash/Rejected) [^10][^11][^12].
   Re-prioritising the merged story is the PO's decision at "Story priorisieren & in Ready-Spalte stellen".

**Checklist**

- Survivor title still names the whole scope; absorbed titles listed as bullets [^2].
- One estimate for the merged story, given by the team [^3].
- Still fits one iteration, else split vertically [^6][^8].
- Absorbed card closed with reason, not left as a second active item [^12].

**Pitfalls**

- Treating the merged card as a detailed specification; keep it a token for a conversation [^13].
- Splitting an oversized merged story by technical layers: neither part is useful to users on its own [^7].
- Merging so much that an unestimatable epic results [^6].

**Not answered by the notebook:** no explicit rule for moving acceptance criteria or feedback links between cards,
and no rule on re-estimating beyond the team estimate (the "sum of estimates need not match" statement is made for
splitting, not for merging [^14]).
