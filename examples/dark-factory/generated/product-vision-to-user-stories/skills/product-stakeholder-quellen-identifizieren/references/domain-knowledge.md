# Domain knowledge — Stakeholder & Anforderungsquellen identifizieren

- Identify user roles across the whole interaction space first, before condensing them into personas; name each role concretely instead of "a user" [^1].
- Generic roles invite pet features and unjustified scope, so every stakeholder row needs a concrete role title [^2].
- The full panel built in 2.1.3 has 6 personas: 4 target users covering different segments, contexts and maturity levels, 1 Contrarian and 1 Verweigerer (Gedächtnis §7.1). The slot allocation here is its seed.
- Contextual inquiry is out of reach in dark mode (Gedächtnis §2.1); the extreme-user idea is covered by the Contrarian and Verweigerer slots.
- Every panel trait later needs SRC grounding with at least 3 source types (Gedächtnis §7.2), so the matrix should already name missing source types per group.

[^1]: "User Stories Applied" (Cohn 2004) — "Rather than writing stories like 'A user can restrict job searches…' you can write 'A Geographic Searcher can restrict his job searches…'"
[^2]: "Fifty Quick Ideas to Improve Your User Stories" (Adzic et al. 2014) — "Using generic roles is often a get-out-of-jail-free card for people who want to push in pet features, or unjustified scope."
