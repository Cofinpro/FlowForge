# Domain knowledge — Pains & Gains extrahieren

- Pains and gains are derived from contradictions between Say/Do and Think/Feel in the empathy map [^1].
- Needs are verbs, not nouns; nouns are usually solutions — the same applies to pains and gains [^1].
- Gedächtnis §10.1: PAIN / GAIN items originate in 2.2.2; §10.2: PAIN is a recommended additional edge in the trace chain.
- Personas carry top-2 pains and top-2 gains (rubric persona), which this list must contain.
- §6: each item carries exactly one evidence level; with mixed support the strongest wins (🔗 > 🤖 > 🧠) and all refs are listed.
- Pains and gains feed POV/HMW (2.2.3) and journey hot spots (3.1.2), so a solution-laden pain propagates downstream.

[^1]: "The Design Thinking Toolbox" (Lewrick et al. 2020) — "The customer tasks are written down according to the pattern: 'When I … (situation), I want to… (motivation), so I can… (expected result).'" / "Use verbs, not nouns. Needs are verbs … Nouns are usually solutions."
