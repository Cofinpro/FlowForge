# Domain knowledge — Hot Spots & Friktionspunkte markieren

- Journey maps record actions, touchpoints, thoughts and an emotion curve per step; hot spots and moments of truth are marked on that curve [^1][^2].
- The journey is outside-in and may start well before and end well after any contact with the product, so hot spots can sit outside our touchpoints [^1].
- Dark-factory rule: every hot spot is either addressed in the future state or skipped with a reason (rubric `journey`), so each HS needs a stable id (`HS-<nnn>`, Gedächtnis §10.1).
- HS, PAIN and ASM are recommended extra edges on the mandatory trace chain (Gedächtnis §10.2).
- Any score discloses inputs and formula so the critic can recompute it (Gedächtnis §2.5, rubric `scoring`).

[^1]: "The Journey Mapping Playbook" (Angrave 2020) — "The customer journey is not about your process maps. Their journey may start well before any contact with you and may carry on well beyond their last interaction with you."
[^2]: "The Design Thinking Toolbox" (Lewrick et al. 2020) — "Define what happens BEFORE, DURING, and AFTER the actual experience … Supplement what the persona thinks … and the emotion he/she feels."
