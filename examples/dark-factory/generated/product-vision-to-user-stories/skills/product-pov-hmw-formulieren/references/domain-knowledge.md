# Domain knowledge — Point of View & How-Might-We formulieren

- One-sentence POV: "[Persona] needs a way to [need] because [surprising insight]", then several HMW questions [^1].
- Pass: the need is a verb and not a feature (needs are verbs, nouns are usually solutions); HMW questions are neither too broad nor too narrow [^1].
- Rubric pov-hmw (Gedächtnis §13): solution-neutral; focused on one persona or one need.
- A POV insight must be surprising, i.e. come from a Say/Do vs. Think/Feel contradiction in the empathy map, not restate the need.
- HMWs become opportunities in the Opportunity-Solution-Tree (2.2.4), so a solution inside an HMW collapses the tree to one branch.
- §10.2: each HMW keeps derivedFrom to its PAIN/GAIN so the chain to JOB and GOAL stays traversable.

[^1]: "The Design Thinking Toolbox" (Lewrick et al. 2020) — "The customer tasks are written down according to the pattern: 'When I … (situation), I want to… (motivation), so I can… (expected result).'" / "Use verbs, not nouns. Needs are verbs … Nouns are usually solutions."
