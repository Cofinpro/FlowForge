# Domain knowledge — Opportunity-Solution-Tree aufbauen

- Tree: Outcome → Opportunities (customer needs) → Solutions → Experiments; every solution hangs under an opportunity, never directly under the goal, and each opportunity has several competing solutions [^1].
- Solutions derived from opportunities are tied to the goal they serve [^1].
- Rubric ost (Gedächtnis §13, Torres): opportunities are phrased from the user's perspective.
- §10.2 mandatory chain: … → (OPP | JOB) → IMP → GOAL → VIS.
- The outcome root is the One Metric That Matters placed on AARRR in business-goals (adopted for 1.2.1).
- Opportunities come from HMW questions, which are already solution-neutral and focused on one persona or need.
- Experiments test the riskiest assumption of a solution; kill assumptions live in the assumptions-map as ASM items.

[^1]: "The Product Manager's Playbook" (Richter 2023) — "You can derive solutions from opportunities, and you know exactly which of your goals they relate to."
