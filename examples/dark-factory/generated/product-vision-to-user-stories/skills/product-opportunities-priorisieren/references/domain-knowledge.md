# Domain knowledge — Opportunities priorisieren

- Opportunity scoring (adopted 2026-09-25): Opportunity = Importance + max(Importance − Satisfaction, 0); Importance and Satisfaction are 1–5 ratings from the panel in rating mode; disclose the inputs so the critic can recompute them [^1].
- Solutions that meet jobs that are important but unsatisfied have a higher chance of succeeding (Ulwick) [^1].
- The tree being prioritised has several competing solutions per opportunity, each tied to a goal [^2].
- Gedächtnis §2.5 and rubric scoring: formula and input values disclosed, recomputable, ranking consistent with the values.
- §9.2: Contrarian and Verweigerer do not count toward the majority, but their objections must be answered; §9.3: panel spread ≥ 2 points signals disagreement.

[^1]: "Mapping Experiences" (Kalbach 2020) — "Solutions that meet unmet needs—or jobs that are important but unsatisfied—have a higher chance of succeeding … a specific technique developed by Tony Ulwick."
[^2]: "The Product Manager's Playbook" (Richter 2023) — "You can derive solutions from opportunities, and you know exactly which of your goals they relate to."
