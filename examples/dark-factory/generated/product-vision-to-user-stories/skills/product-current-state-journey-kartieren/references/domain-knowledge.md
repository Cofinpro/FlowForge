# Domain knowledge — Current-State-Journey kartieren

- Pick a persona and a scenario or job; lay out the stages Before, During and After; for each step record actions, touchpoints, thoughts and an emotion curve; mark hot spots and moments of truth [^1][^2].
- Pass: outside-in (what the user experiences, not internal process steps); end to end, starting before first contact and ending after the transaction; every action tied to a touchpoint or channel [^1][^2].
- Pitfall: confusing a process map with a journey map [^1].
- Dark-factory rule: panel `walkthrough` comments on each journey step (Gedächtnis §7.5); every hot spot is either addressed in the future state or skipped with a reason (rubric `journey`).
- Walkthrough output = one comment per step and persona; synthetic content is always shown as 🤖 and never passed off as cited (Gedächtnis §6, §7.5).

[^1]: "The Journey Mapping Playbook" (Angrave 2020) — "The customer journey is not about your process maps. Their journey may start well before any contact with you and may carry on well beyond their last interaction with you."
[^2]: "The Design Thinking Toolbox" (Lewrick et al. 2020) — "Define what happens BEFORE, DURING, and AFTER the actual experience … Supplement what the persona thinks … and the emotion he/she feels."
