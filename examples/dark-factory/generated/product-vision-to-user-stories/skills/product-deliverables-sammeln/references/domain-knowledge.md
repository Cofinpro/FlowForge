# Domain knowledge — Deliverables als Optionen sammeln (What)

## Deliverables and the impact map (1.2.4)

- Four levels in order: Goal (why, a measurable KPI) → Actors (who) → Impacts (how) → Deliverables (what: options, not commitments) [^1].
- Capture user stories, epics, tasks, product ideas — all the deliverables that could potentially cause a positive impact — and treat them
  as options, not as commitments [^1].
- Pass: deliverables can be dropped once the impact target is reached [^1].
- Pitfall: deliverables treated as immutable [^1].

[^1]: "Fifty Quick Ideas to Improve Your User Stories" (Adzic, Evans, Korac 2014) — "capture user stories, epics, tasks, product ideas – all the deliverables that could potentially cause a positive impact … Then treat them as options, not as commitments."
