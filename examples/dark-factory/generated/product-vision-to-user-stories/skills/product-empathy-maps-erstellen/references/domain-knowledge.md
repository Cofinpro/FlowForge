# Domain knowledge — Empathy Maps erstellen

- Four quadrants from interview notes: Think & Feel, See, Hear, Say & Do; pains and gains are derived afterwards, from contradictions between Say/Do and Think/Feel [^1].
- Pass: Say & Do holds verbatim quotes (🤖 synthetic, marked as such); needs are verbs, not nouns — nouns are usually solutions [^1].
- Gedächtnis §7.5: rating mode produces an item × persona table (1–5); §9.4: steps with optional panel are skipped first when budget runs low, so the map must stand without it.
- Personas are built only from quotes and observed behaviour, so every quadrant entry points back to a transcript (T id).
- §6: Say & Do quotes are 🤖 synthetic; Think & Feel entries are inferences 🧠 and must name the quotes they rest on.
- Contradictions such as "says it is minor, spends hours on the workaround" are the pains and gains S2.2.2 extracts.

[^1]: "The Design Thinking Toolbox" (Lewrick et al. 2020) — "The customer tasks are written down according to the pattern: 'When I … (situation), I want to… (motivation), so I can… (expected result).'" / "Use verbs, not nouns. Needs are verbs … Nouns are usually solutions."
