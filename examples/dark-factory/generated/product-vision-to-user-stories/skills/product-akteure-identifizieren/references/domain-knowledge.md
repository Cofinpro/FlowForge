# Domain knowledge — Akteure identifizieren (Who)

## Actors (1.2.2)

- Four levels in order: Goal (why) → Actors (who: primary, secondary, off-stage) → Impacts (how) → Deliverables (what) [^1].

## Binding rules (Gedächtnis)

- Rubric `impact-map`: strict hierarchy Goal → Actor → Impact → Deliverable (§13).
- Fake story (§10.2): a story whose role is the own team (e.g. "As a developer …") has no user value; avoid own-team actors at the root.
- The proto panel (1.1.4) is grounded in DR-01 SRC evidence and can back actor descriptions (§7.1).

[^1]: "Fifty Quick Ideas to Improve Your User Stories" (Adzic, Evans, Korac 2014) — "capture user stories, epics, tasks, product ideas – all the deliverables that could potentially cause a positive impact … Then treat them as options, not as commitments."
