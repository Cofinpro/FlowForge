# Domain knowledge — Jobs-to-be-Done formulieren

- Syntax: "When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [expected result]" [^1].
- Pass: solution-independent (no UI, no technology); captures emotional and social jobs next to functional ones; needs are verbs, nouns are usually solutions [^1].
- Gedächtnis §10.2: the mandatory chain runs AC → ST → UT → EP/ACTV → (OPP | JOB) → IMP → GOAL → VIS, so each JOB should reach an IMP.
- §9.3: a JOB set with < 50 % 🔗 evidence sends G-P2 to "more research".
- §6: JOB statements condensed from panel answers are 🤖 synthetic; a JOB also backed by a VoC source from the DR-02 corpus is 🔗.
- Jobs are taken from the persona's 1–3 JTBD (rubric persona) and must stay consistent with them.

[^1]: "The Design Thinking Toolbox" (Lewrick et al. 2020) — "The customer tasks are written down according to the pattern: 'When I … (situation), I want to… (motivation), so I can… (expected result).'" / "Use verbs, not nouns. Needs are verbs … Nouns are usually solutions."
