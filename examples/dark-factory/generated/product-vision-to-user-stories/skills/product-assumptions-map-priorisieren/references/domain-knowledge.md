# Domain knowledge — Annahmen in Assumptions Map priorisieren

## Assumptions map (1.1.6)

- Extract the assumptions behind the canvases, classify them as Desirability, Feasibility or Viability, and plot Importance × Evidence.
  High importance with low evidence is the kill zone [^1].
- Pitfall: testing only technical feasibility and ignoring desirability and viability risk [^1].

## Binding rules (Gedächtnis)

- All 🧠 fields of the idea brief are carried over automatically as ASM items (§14).
- No-Go at G-P1/G-P2: at least one kill assumption (importance high, evidence low) is actively refuted by 🔗 evidence (§9.3).
- The interview guide must cover the kill assumptions (§13 `interview-guide`); every story lists the unvalidated ASM on its path (§10.2).

[^1]: "Testing Business Ideas" (Bland, Osterwalder 2019), via notebook answer 2026-09-25 — "Categorize into three risk domains: Desirability, Feasibility, and Viability. Plot on a 2x2 grid of Importance vs. Evidence."
